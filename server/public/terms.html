Terms for ideas and bug reports

Version 2026-10 · in force since 12 October 2026 · Español

1. Who can send

Only people aged 18 or older. If you give a child's name, you declare that you are their parent or guardian, or that you have their permission.

2. What happens to your idea

Sending an idea is voluntary and free. By sending it you give us permission, with no limit in time or place and without exclusivity, to use it, change it, combine it with others, show it inside the game, or not use it. You can keep using your idea however you like.

3. No money and no rights over the game

Sending an idea, having it voted on or having it built does not entitle you to money, prizes or compensation of any kind, and does not make you an author, owner or partner of Zoo Esponji. The game, its name, its code and its art remain the property of their owners.

4. Similar ideas

We may already be working on something similar, or someone else may send the same thing. The game including something like your idea does not mean we took it from you.

5. Votes

We choose which ideas go to a vote and how they are worded. The vote guides what we do next but does not bind us: a winning idea may be delayed, changed or not built if it turns out not to be feasible.

6. Names

The name is optional. We only show first names or nicknames; we may shorten them, change them or not show them. You can ask us to remove a name by writing to android@davidpladel.com.

7. What not to send

Do not send surnames, addresses, phone numbers, photos or other personal data, or material that is not yours. Anything that breaks this is deleted.

8. Your data

We explain it on the form page and in the privacy policy. Messages are deleted after 90 days.

9. Changes

If we change these terms, we will publish the new version here with its date. Each submission is governed by the version it accepted. Earlier versions: none yet.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by Spanish law.
