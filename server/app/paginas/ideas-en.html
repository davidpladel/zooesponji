This page is for adults. Here you can suggest an idea for Zoo Esponji, report a bug, or both. Español

What we do with what you send. Controller: David Salvador Plaza Delmarés (davidpladel), android@davidpladel.com. We use your message only to improve the game and, if you gave a name, to show it next to the idea inside the game. We do this because you send it and accept it. We do not ask for email or surname, we do not store your IP address with your message and we do not share this data with anyone. Messages are deleted after 90 days. You can ask us to delete yours sooner, or to remove a name from the game, by writing to that address. Like any website, the server keeps visits for a few days in technical logs, used only to run it and keep it secure. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es). More in the privacy policy.
