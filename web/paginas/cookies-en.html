Cookie policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What cookies are

Small files that a website stores in your browser to remember something between visits.

Which cookies this website uses

Only its own analytics cookies, from Matomo, a statistics tool installed on our own server. They are not set until you press "Accept" in the cookie notice; if you press "Reject", none are set and the website works the same. We do not use advertising or social media cookies, and the data is never shared.

CookieWhat it is forDuration
_pk_idTells one browser from another to count visits, with a random number that does not say who you are.13 months
_pk_sesGroups the pages you see in one visit.30 minutes
_pk_refRemembers which website you came from, if any.6 months

Your browser also stores your choice (accept or reject) on this device for 12 months, so we do not ask on every page. It is not a cookie and never leaves your browser.

How to change your mind

Press "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page and choose again. If you reject them after accepting, we delete the Matomo cookies. You can also delete or block them in your browser settings.
