Before sending, take a look at the basic data protection information

Controller: David Salvador Plaza Delmarés (davidpladel), creator of Zoo Esponji.

Purpose: to read your idea or bug report to improve the game and, if you gave a first name or nickname, to show it next to the idea inside the game.

Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the boxes. You can withdraw it at any time.

Recipients: we do not share your data with anyone. The message is stored on our server and reaches us as a notice by email and Telegram, used only to let us know.

Retention: messages are deleted after 90 days. We do not ask for email or surname and we do not store your IP address with the message.

Rights: you can ask to access, correct, restrict or delete your message, or to remove a name from the game, by writing to android@davidpladel.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

More information: in the privacy policy.
