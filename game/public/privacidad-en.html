Zoo Esponji · Privacy and terms of use

Last updated: 8 October 2026

In a nutshell

Zoo Esponji is a game for children and it does not collect personal data. There is no sign-up and no need to type a name, and the game has no ads, purchases, chat or social networks. It only counts, anonymously, how much and how the game is played; you can turn that off in Settings.

What the game saves

Your game: the coins, the animals and enclosures you have unlocked, the pages of the book and the settings (music, sounds, joystick, stats and language). Also the first and the last day you played and how many times the game has been opened. So that nothing is counted twice, the device also keeps, only on the device, a small note of what has already been counted (today, this week and ever) and of the time played, and the notices still waiting to be sent; turning "Stats" off deletes the waiting notices. Everything is saved on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Anonymous statistics

To learn what players enjoy and where the game fails, the game sends notices about how it is used to a server owned by davidpladel: that it was opened, which screens are seen, which zoo animal is fed and with which food, which enclosures are visited, which pages of the book are read, what is bought in the in-game shop, and whether there was an error. These are always characters and objects from the game, never data about the player. It uses no cookies and no number that identifies the device or the person. To count how many people play, the device itself remembers whether it has already sent today's notice and does not send it again; that note never leaves the device.

If the game is played offline, the notices wait on the device and are sent when the connection returns; those that could not be sent within about a day are discarded the next time the game is opened. The internet address is shortened on arrival and is only used to know the country. The data is not shared with anyone, is not used for advertising and is deleted after 25 months at the latest. You can turn it off at any time with the "Stats" button in Settings; turning it off also deletes anything that was waiting.

Permissions

The game does not use the device's camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

The game is designed for children aged 6 to 9 and can be played without giving any data. We do not ask for or store information that identifies minors.

Your rights

Since the game does not collect personal data, we hold no data about you to look up, correct or delete; the anonymous statistics can be turned off in Settings. Even so, if you have any question you can write to android@davidpladel.com, and you can always contact the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
